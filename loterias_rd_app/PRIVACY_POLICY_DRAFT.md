# Privacy Policy Draft - Sorteos Dominicanos

Effective date: 2026-09-10

Sorteos Dominicanos is an informational app that displays lottery results from publicly available sources.

## Data collection

The app does not require account registration and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, precise location, contacts, photos, or files.

## Network access

The app connects to `https://api.sorteosdominicanos.workers.dev/results` to load lottery result data.

## Local storage

The app may store user preferences such as favorite lotteries or favorite results locally on the device.

## Payments and gambling

The app does not allow betting, buying tickets, making payments, receiving prizes, or participating in gambling activities.

## Third-party services

The backend may retrieve publicly available lottery result information from external sources. Users should verify final results with official lottery providers.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through the support email configured in Google Play Console.
